Ordinary clue
  1. Controlling team selects a tile.
  2. Clue appears publicly; configurable timer defaults to 15 seconds.
  3. Host clicks a team or presses its number key.
  4. Correct: add tile value, play applause, and give that team control.
  5. Incorrect: subtract tile value, play the miss cue, lock that team out, and allow another team to answer.
  6. When time expires or nobody remains, reveal the accepted response, explanation, and source.
Daily Double and Final

Daily Double: only the selecting team answers. Its wager may be up to its score or the round’s highest clue value, whichever is greater. Correct adds; incorrect subtracts.

Final: reveal category, collect private written wagers and answers, run a 30-second timer, then reveal teams from lowest to highest pre-Final score. Correct adds the wager; incorrect subtracts it.

Tie: one sudden-death clue; first correct response wins.

HOST SAFETY NET

Autosave after every action · Undo last action · Manual score adjustment with reason · Pause/reset timer · Reopen an accidentally closed clue · Skip/report a bad clue.

TWO DISPLAY MODES

Dual-screen: laptop shows answers, sources, timers, and controls; projector shows only the game. Single-screen fallback: host controls appear in a collapsible lower panel.
